OpenDash Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

OpenDash is an independent motorcycle companion app for route preview, ride history, vehicle records, garage maintenance, fuel and expense tracking, themes, and downloadable phone wallpapers.

1. Information OpenDash uses

  • Location: Used for route preview, ride tracking, distance, speed, and related ride features.
  • Vehicle and garage data: Vehicle profiles, service details, maintenance intervals, odometer readings, and reminders entered by you.
  • Fuel and expense data: Fuel entries, ownership costs, categories, and notes entered by you.
  • Ride data: Ride distance, duration, speed, route-related data, and ride history.
  • Diagnostics: Limited technical information may be processed when optional diagnostic services are enabled. Release builds should avoid recording precise coordinates, full shared URLs, account identifiers, or device identifiers.

OpenDash does not pair with, authenticate to, project content onto, or control motorcycle dashboard hardware. It does not request or store motorcycle-dashboard credentials.

2. Local-only mode

OpenDash is designed to work locally without requiring an account. Vehicle records, garage data, fuel entries, expenses, ride history, route preview, themes, and downloadable phone wallpapers can be used without signing in.

3. Optional sync

Some builds may support optional Google sign-in and Firebase sync when configured with a Firebase project. If you choose to sign in, supported application records may be processed by the configured Firebase services for backup or cross-device sync. Without sign-in or Firebase configuration, your data remains local to your device.

4. How information is used

  • Providing route preview and ride tracking.
  • Showing vehicle, garage, fuel, maintenance, ride, and expense history.
  • Providing optional account backup and synchronization.
  • Diagnosing application failures when optional diagnostics are enabled.

5. Sharing of information

OpenDash does not sell user data or share personal vehicle, ride, garage, or expense records with advertisers. If optional Google or Firebase services are enabled, information required for account and synchronization functionality may be processed by those providers under their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Data storage and security

Application data is stored locally unless optional sync is enabled. Protect your device with a secure lock screen and remove personal information from logs or screenshots before sharing them.

7. Data deletion

You can remove local data using available in-app delete or reset controls, or by clearing OpenDash storage or uninstalling the application. If optional cloud sync is enabled, contact the developer to request deletion of synchronized account data.

8. Children

OpenDash is intended for motorcycle riders and is not directed at children.

9. Safety notice

Do not interact with the application while riding. Configure routes and application settings before starting a ride. Always follow local traffic laws.

10. Changes to this policy

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a revised date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, use the support channel published with the application or repository.
